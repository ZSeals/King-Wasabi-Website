Location Overview

Tybee Island is a small coastal island located about 20–25 minutes from Savannah. It’s known for its relaxed atmosphere, wide sandy beaches, and historic landmarks like the lighthouse and Fort Pulaski. Unlike crowded tourist beaches, Tybee has a more local and laid-back feel, making it ideal for people who want a quieter waterfront experience.

Attractions

  • Relax on North, Mid, or South Beach
  • Visit the Tybee Island Lighthouse & Museum
  • Go dolphin watching or take a boat tour
  • Kayak through marshes or paddleboard
  • Walk along the pier and watch fishermen
  • Bike around the island or explore trails
  • Visit the Marine Science Center
  • Try local seafood restaurants
